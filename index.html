Symptom information

Seek urgent medical help
Some combinations of symptoms can require prompt assessment. If symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening, or you feel seriously unwell, seek urgent medical help. In the UK, call 999 for a life-threatening emergency; NHS 111 can help with urgent advice when it is not an emergency.
